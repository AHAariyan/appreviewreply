## r/androiddev (check the weekly "self-promotion" thread rules first; otherwise post as a discussion)
Title: I built an app that drafts replies to my Play Store reviews in my own voice — looking for 10 indie devs to beta test
Body:
Replying to reviews moves ratings (people update their stars), but with a few apps it's an hour a day, so I mostly didn't. Team tools that draft replies with AI start at $219/month.
So I built AppReviewReply: sign in with the Google account you use for Play Console, add your package names, and every new review shows up in an inbox with a draft reply in your tone (from a few example replies you paste in), under Google's 350-char limit. You edit, tap Post, it goes through the official API. Reviews mentioning crashes/bugs/feature requests get tagged into an Issues tab per version.
It's Android-only, nothing is posted automatically, reviews stay on your phone. Free during beta; planned $9/mo after.
Screenshots + beta link: https://appreviewreply.app
Question: what would make you stop replying by hand — the drafting, the bug tagging, or the daily notification?
