**App name (30):** AppReviewReply

**Short description (80):**
Reply to your Play Store reviews in your own voice. Drafts, bug tags, daily inbox.

**Full description (4000):**
AppReviewReply is for independent Android developers who know replying to reviews matters — users update their ratings, Google notices — but never find the hour a day it takes.

Sign in with the Google account you already use for Play Console, add your package names, and every new review lands in an inbox. Open one and a reply is drafted for you: in your tone (from a few example replies you paste in), specific to what the reviewer said, under Google's 350-character limit. Edit it or not, tap Post, and it goes out through the official Google Play Developer API as your reply.

Reviews that mention crashes, bugs or feature requests are tagged automatically and collected in the Issues tab, grouped by app version, so nothing users tell you gets lost in the stars.

What it does
• Daily inbox of new reviews across all your apps — unanswered first, low ratings first
• One-tap reply drafts in your voice, any language the review is written in
• Automatic tagging: crash, bug, feature request, praise, question, complaint
• Issues tab per app version
• Notification when new reviews arrive
• Works with your existing Play Console permissions — no service accounts, no JSON keys

What it doesn't do
• It never posts automatically. You are always the author and the last click.
• It never touches anything in Play Console except reviews and replies.

Privacy
Reviews and replies stay on your phone. A review's text is sent to the drafting service only when you ask for a draft and is not stored there. Bring your own API key if you prefer drafts billed to your own account. Full policy: appreviewreply.app/privacy.html

Pricing
Free while in beta. Afterwards $9/month or $79/year for all your apps, with 500 drafts a month included.
