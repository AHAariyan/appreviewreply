How it works

1. Sign in with Google

The same account you use for Play Console. Add the package names of your apps. No service accounts, no JSON keys.

2. Open your inbox

New reviews arrive daily with a notification. Unanswered first, 1–2 star first.

3. Draft, edit, post

A reply in your tone, under Google's 350-character limit, written from your example replies. One tap to post through the official API.

4. Never lose a bug

Reviews mentioning crashes, bugs or feature requests are tagged and collected per app version in the Issues tab.

Questions

Does it post replies automatically?

No. Every reply is drafted, shown to you, and posted only when you tap Post. You are always the author.

What permissions does it need?

Your Google account's "Reply to reviews" permission in Play Console, granted through Google's standard sign-in screen. The app reads reviews with text from the last 7 days (the window Google's API provides) and keeps history on your phone.

Where is my data stored?

Reviews and replies stay on your device. A review's text is sent to our drafting service only when you request a draft; it is not stored there. See the privacy policy.

Which platforms?

Android app, for Google Play reviews. App Store reviews are planned.
